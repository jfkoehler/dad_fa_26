\begin{frame}{Data Hunt}
\redrule
\begin{columns}[T]
\column{0.52\textwidth}
{\Large\bfseries Find real data that could help investigate a question.}

\vspace{0.35cm}
You are \alert{not} looking for an article that answers the question. You are looking for data that would let you investigate it yourself.

\column{0.43\textwidth}
\begin{block}{Ask}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Who collected it?
  \item What does one row represent?
  \item What variables are available?
  \item What could it help answer?
  \item What's missing?
\end{itemize}
\end{block}
\end{columns}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{What Is Missing?}
\redrule
\begin{itemize}
  \setlength\itemsep{0.35cm}
  \item Who or what might be absent from this dataset?
  \item What decisions were made before you ever encountered the file?
  \item What categories were chosen?
  \item What was measured---and what was not?
  \item What should someone know before using this data to make a claim or decision?
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}
